# Copied from the README's Label Taxonomy so baseline.py is judged on exactly
# the definitions written there. baseline.py reads this file.

problem: reports a current physical symptom such as blood, pain, swelling, wetness, a tear, a prolapse or a blowout
check: wants a verdict on whether something is okay, normal, safe or possible, and reports no current symptom
practical: wants information, instructions or a product rather than a verdict
share: shows a result, story or setup and asks for nothing
